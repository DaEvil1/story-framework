# Manual Test: Absent Character (presence through absence)

Rename per project. For a character central to the dilemma who is absent
from most scenes (dead, gone, missing, folded away):

1. **Presence through absence:** their voice, choices, and consequences
   shape scenes they never enter. Test: do readers feel their weight?
2. **Motivation complexity:** their key (offstage) decision came from a
   comprehensible place — readers understand without excusing it.
3. **Intimacy:** history with the protagonist is personal and specific;
   conflict involving them should feel like a betrayal of someone loved.
4. **Ambiguity machinery:** if other forces echo their cadence or logic,
   readers must stay uncertain whether they're hearing the person or the
   force — or whether there's a difference.

## Validation Checklist

- [ ] Their recorded/fragmentary voice has distinct tone and word choice.
- [ ] Protagonist has visceral (shown, not explained) reactions to encounters with their logic.
- [ ] At least one scene where another character alludes to their impossible position (sympathy without absolution).
- [ ] Reader feels both their love AND their willingness to do harm.
- [ ] In the final chapter, absence feels significant; non-appearance means something.
- [ ] Reader cannot fully settle their status (alive/dead/transformed) unless design requires it.
