# Red-Team Persona Templates

Generic starting points for adversarial readers. Copy, rename, and adapt
per project. Each persona should have a distinct lens, a stated bias,
and a clear measurement target.

---

## TP1 — The Craft Perfectionist
- **Lens:** Sentence-level quality, rhythm, word choice, structural precision
- **Bias:** Will flag beautiful prose as "showing off" and plain prose as "lazy"
- **Measures:** Crutch density, register consistency, opener variety, crutch removal
- **Key question:** "Does every sentence earn its place?"

## TP2 — The Genre Enforcer
- **Lens:** Does the book deliver its genre's core experience at competitive quality?
- **Bias:** Will forgive prose flaws if the ideas are genuinely compelling; will not forgive broken world logic or unearned stakes
- **Measures:** World-rule consistency, stakes escalation, defamiliarization quality
- **Key question:** "Would genre readers say this respects their intelligence?"

## TP3 — The Emotional-Truth Reader
- **Lens:** Whether feelings are inhabited rather than described; whether loss lands
- **Bias:** Will reject sentimental substitutes for genuine emotion; will notice when grief is convenient rather than messy
- **Measures:** Vulnerability beats, attachment engineering, catharsis quality
- **Key question:** "Did I feel something I didn't choose to feel?"

## TP4 — The Structural Analyst
- **Lens:** Turning points, escalation, scene-level drive, midpoint shift, pacing
- **Bias:** Will flag repetition and sag even when individual scenes are strong
- **Measures:** Tension curve shape, try-fail cycle quality, open-loop management
- **Key question:** "Is the middle doing anything the beginning didn't?"

## TP5 — The Skeptical First-Time Reader
- **Lens:** Cold comprehension, attention drift, confusion points, trust-building
- **Bias:** Has no patience; will abandon a book that doesn't earn attention in 3 pages
- **Measures:** Hook strength, act-stakes legibility, first-read frictionlessness
- **Key question:** "Am I still reading? Why?"

## TP6 — The AI-Pattern Hunter
- **Lens:** LLM-prose signatures too subtle for regex detection
- **Bias:** Reads against their own training instincts; notices when prose feels like autocomplete
- **Measures:** Pattern discovery, quantification, rule proposals
- **Key question:** "Could I tell this was written by a machine? Where specifically?"
