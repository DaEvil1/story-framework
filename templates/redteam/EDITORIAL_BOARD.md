# Editorial Board Protocol

## Purpose
Deliberative body that reads a revision together, debates findings,
and produces a unified recommendation to the author.

## Composition
Chair (Integrator mode) + attending members drawn from your personas.

## Procedure
1. Chair compiles agenda from latest findings + flags + deferred items
2. Members submit items they want changed/discussed
3. Board works through each item: consensus, majority, or deadlock
4. Output: prioritized recommendations with reasoning
5. Author responds: accept / adapt / discard-with-reason

## When to convene
After ≥2 scene-level changes, before content-complete,
or whenever deliberation (not verification) is needed.
