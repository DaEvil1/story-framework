This document captures the creative vision, emotional arc, and thematic
conclusions you want readers to reach naturally through the story. It's
*not* prose — it's the internal North Star for why choices get made in the
chapters.

**Access discipline:** intent docs are hidden from cold readers (red-team,
council, reader-state snapshots). The communication check exists precisely
to diff what readers take away against what you intended.

## How This Guides Writing

When drafting:
- **Ask:** What should the reader understand about the protagonist's internal state without being told? Find a physical action, dialogue, or sensory detail that communicates it.
- **Ask:** What thematic beat belongs here? Show it through a scene where it emerges naturally from character conflict.
- **Ask:** What false resolution is tempting? Show why it won't work; push toward the harder, more integrated choice.

The prose should *enact* these beats, never *explain* them.
