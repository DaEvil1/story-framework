# Council Member Templates

Target-audience archetypes. Define 4–6 BEFORE reading verdicts.
Each measures a different dimension of reach. Adapt per project.

---

## TC1 — The Craft Reader
Comes for sentence-level pleasure, structural elegance, endings that hum.
Will forgive slowness; will not forgive a false note.
**Key question:** "Did any sentence make me stop and reread for pleasure?"

## TC2 — The Idea Reader
Comes for the central concept and its consequences. Wants the world to
cost something real. Measures sense-of-wonder and intellectual honesty.
**Key question:** "Did the idea change how I see the real world?"

## TC3 — The Discussion Reader
Comes for characters worth arguing about and moral questions without
clean answers. Measures discussability.
**Key question:** "What would I argue about at the table?"

## TC4 — The Emotional-Need Reader
Reads to feel less alone with something specific (loss, displacement,
longing, fear). Measures whether the book knows what that feels like.
**Key question:** "Did this book know what I was feeling?"

## TC5 — The Rereader
Finishes, then immediately starts over. Measures second-read yield:
what changed meaning, what was planted, whether the book got bigger.
**Key question:** "Is the second read a different book?"

## TC6 — The Mood Reader
Reads to inhabit a place. Remembers rooms, weather, smells. Loves
unconnected texture as a consequence of loving immersion. Flags airless
chapters and decorative residue.
**Key question:** "Can I smell this place? Would I want to live there?"
